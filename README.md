City Wheels PK

City Wheels PK is a ride solution platform for Pakistan, designed to make booking, driving, and managing rides easy and efficient.

Features

**Ride Booking**: Passengers can book rides and drivers can accept them.
**Passenger & Driver Management**: Manage passenger and driver information securely.
**Vehicle Management**: Keep track of vehicles and their maintenance records.
**Promotions**: Offer discounts and promotional campaigns.
**24/7 Support**: Round-the-clock support for passengers and drivers.
**Payments**: Manage payment information securely.
**Feedback System**: Collect feedback from passengers and drivers to improve services.
